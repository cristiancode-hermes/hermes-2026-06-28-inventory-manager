# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

warehouse managers and business operators who need real-time inventory visibility

## Product Purpose

Prevent stockouts and overstock through real-time inventory intelligence

## Brand Personality

robust, practical, data-driven

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates with purple-to-blue gradients
- Over-designed dashboards where data is buried in decoration
- AI-generated interfaces that all share one look (Inter font, cream backgrounds, rounded everything)
- Enterprise apps that confuse density with professionalism

## Design Principles

- Stock levels should never be a surprise
- Alerts before problems, not after
- Multi-tenant means isolated data, shared experience
- Purchase orders connect suppliers to stock automatically

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance target
- Proper color contrast ratios (body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1)
- Keyboard navigable interface
- Screen reader friendly semantic HTML
- Support for prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme
